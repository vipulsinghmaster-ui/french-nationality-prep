Apple structure × Duolingo energy

Same Apple layout (large titles, tab bar, safe areas, SF Rounded type, Apple blue/green/red) plus Duolingo’s playful habits: streak + XP header, a learning path, chunky pressable buttons, thick-bordered cards, a lesson flow with a progress bar and a “Vérifier → Bravo !” feedback bar, and a celebration screen. Tap the answers, the tabs and the path.

Inspiration is taken from Duolingo’s publicly described patterns (home path with unit headers, tiered tab headers, white cards on light grey, quests and streak progress). It is not copied: no Duolingo logo, mascot, or font. Fonts: the iPhone uses SF Pro Rounded (ui-rounded); your PC shows a stand-in.
